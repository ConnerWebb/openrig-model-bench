Operation
  Separate user service: openrig-performance-dashboard.service
  Observer checks result files every 10 seconds; open tabs check every 15
  seconds. Finalized results normally appear within about 30 seconds.
  This is a solo-task graph. Team and parallel-inference results are not
  combined into solo scores. Harness revisions remain separate. Coverage
  and Python/C++ sample counts remain visible. No in-progress or interrupted
  trial is counted. h5.5 requires the final resource_limited field after
  cleanup, plus a two-second file-age delay.
  Only result changes produce a new snapshot. A failed read retains the
  last good data and produces a visible error or stale-check warning.
